Banana bread

Ingredients: 3 ripe bananas, 75g melted butter, 150g sugar, 1 egg, 1 teaspoon vanilla, 1 teaspoon baking soda, a pinch of salt and 190g plain flour.

Method: Heat the oven to 175C. Mash the bananas in a bowl and stir in the butter. Mix in the sugar, egg and vanilla, then the baking soda and salt. Fold in the flour. Pour into a greased loaf tin and bake for about an hour until a skewer comes out clean. Cool before slicing.
