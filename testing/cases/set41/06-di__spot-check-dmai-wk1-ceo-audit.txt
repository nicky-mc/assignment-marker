Our vision is to become the most trusted independent bakery in the city. Our mission is to bake fresh bread daily using local ingredients. Our values are quality, community, and honesty.

Applying the 7Ps: Product - fresh sourdough and pastries. Price - mid-range, slightly above supermarket. Place - one shop plus a farmers market stall. Promotion - Instagram posts and word of mouth, no paid ads. People - two bakers and one counter staff. Process - orders taken in person or via a simple online form. Physical Evidence - the shop's rustic decor and smell of fresh bread. We don't currently do much promotion beyond organic social posts, which is a gap I'd like to address with a proper content calendar.
