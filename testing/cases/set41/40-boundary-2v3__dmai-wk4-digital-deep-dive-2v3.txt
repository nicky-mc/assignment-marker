Objectives: Increase online sales by 15% over the next quarter and grow email subscriber list by 200 people, tied to the business's overall growth goal.

Content Strategy: Plan is to post a weekly blog article and three social posts a week, focused on product education and customer stories, matched to where the audience is in the funnel (awareness content on social, consideration content in blog posts).

Channel Strategy: Chosen channels are Instagram and email, since the audience is already active on Instagram and email has historically driven the highest repeat-purchase rate.

Media Strategy: We use owned and earned media mostly, some paid ads occasionally.

Tactics: Weekly email newsletter with a discount code, Instagram Reels showing product use, and a monthly customer spotlight post.

Deep dive - Email: Currently sending one newsletter a month with an open rate of around 18%. Plan is to move to weekly sends, segment the list into new vs. repeat customers, and A/B test subject lines to improve open rates, with the goal of lifting open rate to 25% and click-through rate to 4% by testing send times and personalising the greeting.
