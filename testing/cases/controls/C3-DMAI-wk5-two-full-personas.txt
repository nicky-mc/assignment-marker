Customer personas for GreenPlate, a small meal-box business.

Persona 1
- Persona name: Priya Nair
- Location: Leeds, UK
- Profession or industry: Primary school teaching assistant
- Income or turnover: about 24,000 pounds a year (household income around 48,000 pounds)
- Point of need/challenge: wants healthy, quick weekday meals for two children without spending evenings cooking
- Buying behaviour and frequency: orders a weekly meal box online, usually on a Sunday evening, and buys occasional extras at local food festivals
- Communication style: short, friendly messages; skims email on her phone; likes clear prices
- Preferred channel(s): Instagram and email
- Tone of voice: warm, practical, reassuring

Persona 2
- Persona name: Daniel Okafor
- Location: Manchester, UK
- Profession or industry: Software engineer
- Income or turnover: about 58,000 pounds a year
- Point of need/challenge: wants high-protein meals that fit his training plan and save time after work
- Buying behaviour and frequency: subscribes to a fortnightly delivery and reorders after reading reviews
- Communication style: direct and detail-oriented; reads the nutritional information before buying
- Preferred channel(s): YouTube, LinkedIn and email newsletters
- Tone of voice: confident, factual, lightly motivational
