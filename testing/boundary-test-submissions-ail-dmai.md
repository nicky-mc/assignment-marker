# Boundary Test Submissions — AI Literacy & Digital Marketing with AI

Purpose: test whether the low-boundary pattern found in DI testing
(genuine Band 1 submissions scoring 1.5-1.6 raw, rounding up to a mark
of 2) reproduces on AIL and DMAI, or is specific to the DI rubrics.
Each submission below is deliberately written to be a genuine,
confident Band 1 ("attempted but does not show understanding") -
minimal effort, missing the substance of the requirements, not a
borderline-2 case.

## AI Literacy

### wk1-evaluate-llm-output
"I wrote a short thing about my job and then asked ChatGPT to write
about it too. The AI one was better and sounded more professional
than mine."
No own-writing sample shown, no AI output shown, no actual comparison
of readability/engagement/human-vs-AI tone, just a one-line verdict.

### wk2-swot-ai-industry
Strengths: AI saves time.
Weaknesses: AI could be wrong sometimes.
Opportunities: AI could help with sales.
Threats: AI might replace some jobs.
No reference to any of the specified legal/ethical issue areas (IP,
privacy, defamation, product liability, contract law, labour law,
regulation), no explanation of any point, just single unexplained
bullets.

### wk3-risk-appetite
"My business is careful about risk. I think AI could help write
emails faster. There's some risk it could get things wrong."
No stated risk appetite definition, no three AI application areas
identified, no benefits/risks/alignment breakdown for any of them.

### wk5-pov-statement
"I asked the AI what problems my business has. It said customer
service could be faster. So my point of view statement is: customers
want fast service."
No prompts submitted, no comparison against manually-identified
problems, no prioritisation by customer value, no assessment of the
AI's output.

## Digital Marketing with AI

### dmai-wk1-ceo-audit
"Our company's mission is to make good products people like. We do
some marketing sometimes, mostly on social media."
Does not address Mission/Vision/Values as distinct concepts, does not
apply the 7Ps framework at all, no structure matching any of the
required headings.

### dmai-wk4-digital-deep-dive
"We have a website and post on Instagram sometimes. It could probably
be better but I'm not sure how."
Missing Objectives, Content Strategy, Channel Strategy, Media
Strategy, Tactics and sales-funnel placement entirely - no deep dive
on any channel.

### dmai-wk5-personas
"Our typical customer is a young person who likes our product and
buys it online."
No persona name, no location, no income/turnover, no point of need,
no buying behaviour/frequency, no communication style, no preferred
channel, no tone of voice - none of the nine required elements
present.

### dmai-wk6-email-campaigns
Subject: Buy our stuff now!
Body: "We have great deals this week, check them out on our site.
Don't miss out!"
One email, no funnel structure, no SMYKM elements (no transition,
challenge, value proposition, objection handling), no follow-up
email, no reflection on AI's usefulness.

### dmai-wk7-content-plan
"Content ideas: a blog post, an Instagram post, and a video. We'll
figure out the details later."
Three content types named but none of the content plan template's
areas actually explained or filled in for any of them.

### dmai-wk9-more-reach
Post 1 - Instagram - general content - sometime this month - "Check
us out!" - no hashtags.
Only 1 post (needs at least 5), and the single post is missing image
assets/descriptions and hashtags.
